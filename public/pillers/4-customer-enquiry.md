## 2. How AeroSun should use this form

I recommend a two-stage process rather than asking every customer to complete all 15 sections.

| Stage | Form sections | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Initial enquiry | 1, 2, 3, 4, 10 | During the first customer conversation |
| Technical site survey | 5–13 | After confirming genuine customer interest |
| Customer confirmation | 14 | Before proceeding with detailed proposal |
| Internal approval | 11, 13, 15 | After the site visit |

## 3. Recommended customer enquiry process

**Customer → Enquiry → Initial assessment → Site survey → Technical design → Supplier quotation → Customer proposal → Order confirmation → Installation → Handover**

Maintain a unique reference number for every enquiry:

- ASE-ENQ-0001 – First customer enquiry
- ASE-ENQ-0002 – Second customer enquiry
- ASE-ENQ-0003 – Third customer enquiry

Keep customer contact details, electricity bills and site photographs confidential, and collect only the information necessary for the project.

## 4. My recommendation for the next step

Convert this into a **mobile-friendly online enquiry form using your AeroSun website**, with conditional questions so customers only see the relevant sections for solar, wind, agricultural or hybrid systems. This will let customers submit enquiries without printing or signing a lengthy form.
