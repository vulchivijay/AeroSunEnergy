## 5. Bank finance and MSME credit facilities

Two credit-guarantee programmes deserve attention.

### A. Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS)

This supports eligible loans to DPIIT-recognised startups through participating banks, NBFCs and eligible investment institutions.

The revised framework provides guarantee coverage for eligible debt up to ₹20 crore per borrower.

Important conditions include DPIIT recognition, lender assessment and the absence of default/NPA status. The guarantee is provided to the lending institution, not directly to AeroSun Energy. [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/santhali/content/sih/en/credit-guarantee-scheme-for-startups.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Official information: [Startup India – Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/credit-guarantee-scheme-for-startups.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### B. CGTMSE

The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises supports eligible lending to micro and small enterprises.

It can help eligible businesses obtain loans with reduced collateral requirements, subject to lender assessment and applicable guarantee conditions.

It is not a direct grant or loan waiver. [MSME](https://my.msme.gov.in/mymsme/Scheme.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Official website: [CGTMSE](https://www.cgtmse.in/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**My recommendation:** Approach banks after preparing your project report, supplier quotations, financial projections and promoter-contribution details.
