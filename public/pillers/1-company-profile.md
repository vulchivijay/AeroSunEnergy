## 1. About Us

AeroSun Energy Private Limited is an Indian renewable-energy company focused on developing and delivering practical clean-energy solutions using solar and wind power.

Our business vision is to make renewable energy more accessible, adaptable and useful for households, agricultural operations, commercial establishments and remote communities.

We aim to combine renewable-energy products, customised system design, engineering, equipment integration and technical services to address different energy requirements.

Our long-term direction includes the development of customised small wind generators, solar power systems and integrated solar–wind hybrid solutions.

### B. Wind Energy Solutions

AeroSun Energy is developing a customised small wind-generator product direction, with a proposed capacity range from 100 W to 10 kW.

Our initial engineering development programme focuses on a 3 kW hybrid vertical-axis wind turbine combining Savonius and Darrieus rotor concepts.

Development objectives include:

- Customised power capacities.
- Investigation of low-wind-speed performance.
- Permanent-magnet generator integration.
- Mechanical and electrical system optimisation.
- Performance monitoring and testing.
- Applications for farms, rural locations and distributed energy systems.

These technologies remain subject to engineering development, prototype validation and applicable certification.

### C. Solar–Wind Hybrid Energy

## 6. Our Innovation Focus

AeroSun Energy's product-development direction focuses on addressing important renewable-energy challenges.

Our identified areas of interest include:

1. Limited availability of affordable, customised small wind generators.
2. Inefficient utilisation of low-speed wind resources.
3. Dependence on conventional electricity sources.
4. Intermittent renewable-energy generation.
5. Limited access to integrated solar and wind solutions for rural and agricultural applications.
6. Difficulty accessing reliable renewable energy in remote locations.

We aim to address these challenges through customised product development, renewable-energy integration and practical engineering solutions.
