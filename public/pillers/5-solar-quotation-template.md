# 3. Important commercial advice for AeroSun

Since AeroSun currently operates without its own inventory or installation workforce:

- Obtain supplier quotations before finalising customer prices.
- Include transportation, installation, taxes and project coordination costs.
- Clearly identify who supplies the equipment and who performs installation.
- Do not promise government subsidies unless eligibility and approval are confirmed.
- Do not guarantee electricity generation or savings without a defensible technical assessment.
- Keep a signed customer acceptance and payment record.
- Avoid purchasing equipment before the customer's order and agreed advance payment are secured.
