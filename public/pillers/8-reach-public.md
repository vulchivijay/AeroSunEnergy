For AeroSun Energy Private Limited, I’d use a local trust + education + lead-generation strategy, rather than starting with broad brand advertising.

The strategy I recommend

1. Dominate a small geography first

Since you’re building AeroSun around solar installation, engineering and monitoring, initially focus on specific areas instead of trying to reach all of India.

For example:

Tirupati → Chittoor → Nellore → Bengaluru/Chennai corridor

Your message can be simple:

“AeroSun Energy: Helping homes and businesses reduce electricity costs with smarter solar solutions.”

2. Build 5 customer acquisition channels
   Channel What AeroSun should do Google Search & Maps Create/optimize Google Business Profile and collect genuine installation reviews
   Instagram + Facebook Short educational videos and before/after installations
   YouTube Telugu + English solar education
   WhatsApp Business Quotations, customer enquiries and follow-ups
   
   Local partnerships Builders, electricians, architects, factories, schools and commercial property owners

For a new solar company, I would not spend heavily on branding ads initially. Spend money where you can capture people already thinking about electricity bills or solar.

3. Your strongest marketing weapon: education

AeroSun shouldn't only advertise:

“We install solar panels.”

Instead, answer questions customers are already asking.

Create videos/posts such as:

₹3,000 electricity bill: how much solar do I need?
₹5,000 bill: is 3 kW or 5 kW better?
“How much does a 5 kW solar system save?”
“What happens during cloudy weather?”
“How many years does it take to recover solar investment?”
“On-grid vs off-grid vs hybrid solar”
“How much roof space does 5 kW require?”
“Does solar work during a power cut?”
“How to choose a good solar installer”
“Solar panel warranty vs performance warranty”
“What maintenance does rooftop solar require?”

For your target market, I would make many of these in Telugu, with English subtitles.

7. Make trust highly visible

Solar is a relatively large purchase, so customers need reassurance.

Your website/social profiles should clearly show:

AeroSun Energy Private Limited
actual office/contact information
GST/CIN details where appropriate
installation photographs
panel/inverter brands offered
product warranties
workmanship/service commitments
actual customer testimonials
engineering/site-survey process
monitoring and after-sales support
transparent quotation structure

Avoid generic stock-photo-heavy marketing once you have real AeroSun projects.

8. Create an AeroSun referral program

Solar has excellent word-of-mouth potential.

After installation:

Refer your neighbour/friend to AeroSun. If they complete an installation, receive an AeroSun referral reward.

The exact incentive should depend on your margins, but it could be a cash reward, maintenance package, cleaning service, monitoring upgrade, or other appropriate benefit.

A customer with solar panels is effectively advertising the concept to the entire neighbourhood.

One important positioning idea for AeroSun

Your monitoring/fault-detection concept can eventually become a differentiator.

Instead of positioning AeroSun only as:

Solar Installation Company

build toward:

Smart Solar Energy Company

Design • Installation • Monitoring • Optimization

That gives AeroSun a stronger identity than installers competing almost entirely on panel price.

If I were running AeroSun

My priority would be:

Google visibility → Telugu educational content → Solar Calculator → WhatsApp leads → Real installation case studies → Customer referrals → Local partnerships → Paid advertising

The goal for the first year wouldn't be “make everyone know AeroSun.”

It would be:

“When someone in our target region seriously considers solar, AeroSun should be one of the first companies they find, understand and trust.”
