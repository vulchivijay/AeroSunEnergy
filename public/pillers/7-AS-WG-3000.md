# 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

AeroSun Energy Private Limited proposes to develop AS-WG-3000, a 3 kW hybrid vertical-axis wind generator designed for investigation of low-wind-speed starting performance and distributed renewable-energy applications.

The proposed turbine combines two aerodynamic concepts:

1. A central Savonius rotor intended to assist starting at lower wind speeds.
2. Three external helical Darrieus blades intended to provide the main energy conversion during operation.

The proposed system will use a direct-drive permanent-magnet synchronous generator (PMSG), subject to engineering validation and generator performance matching.

The project aims to investigate the feasibility of a compact, modular and customisable small wind generator for agricultural properties, rural locations, remote installations and potential solar–wind hybrid energy systems.

AeroSun Energy is seeking incubation, technical mentorship, prototype-development funding, access to fabrication facilities and testing support to progress from preliminary design to a validated prototype.

The proposed 3 kW output is a development target. Actual performance, starting wind speed, efficiency, operating range and energy generation will be established through engineering analysis and controlled testing.

# 4. PROPOSED INNOVATION

## 4.1 Hybrid Savonius–Darrieus Vertical-Axis Design

AS-WG-3000 is proposed as a hybrid vertical-axis wind turbine.

The concept combines:

**Savonius Rotor**

- Located centrally within the turbine.
- Intended to improve self-starting behaviour.
- Designed to generate starting torque.
- Proposed as a two-stage arrangement.

**Helical Darrieus Rotor**

- Three external helical blades.
- Intended to provide the primary aerodynamic energy conversion.
- Vertical-axis configuration.
- Designed for investigation of operating performance under varying wind conditions.

## 4.2 Electrical Generation

The proposed electrical system includes:

- Direct-drive permanent-magnet synchronous generator.
- Three-phase electrical output.
- Rectification and electrical protection.
- Appropriate wind turbine controller.
- Overspeed protection and braking arrangements.
- Compatible battery charging or power-conversion interface, subject to system design.

The generator, rectifier, controller and electrical protection system will be selected and matched through engineering calculations and testing.

## 4.3 Intended Innovation Areas

The project will investigate:

1. Hybrid Savonius–Darrieus rotor configuration.
2. Low-wind-speed starting behaviour.
3. Rotor and generator torque-speed matching.
4. Direct-drive low-RPM power generation.
5. Modular and customised small wind-generator design.
6. Potential integration with solar and battery systems.

These are proposed development and validation areas, not claims of established technical novelty or patentability.
