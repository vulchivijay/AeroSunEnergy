### Important procurement recommendation

Do not register every supplier as an approved supplier immediately. Maintain three statuses:

1. **Prospective:** Contacted, registration pending.
2. **Under evaluation:** Documents, technical specifications and quotations received.
3. **Approved:** Business details, product suitability, quality and commercial terms verified.

For your first round, approach approximately 15 suppliers: 8 solar suppliers and 7 wind-component suppliers.

This will help AeroSun build a supplier database without committing to inventory or advance payments.
