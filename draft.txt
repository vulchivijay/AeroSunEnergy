### How to increase the chances of receiving free equipment

I recommend sending this email with a short partnership proposal attached. Manufacturers are more likely to consider the request when they can see a measurable return.

| What you request | What you offer |
|---|---|
| Free solar demonstration setup | Product videos, website promotion and customer demonstrations |
| Free wind turbine demonstration unit | Wind product promotion and regional lead generation |
| Free hybrid demonstration setup | Demonstration of integrated solar, wind and storage solutions |
| Technical training | Local customer education and product awareness |
| Special equipment pricing | Future project orders and regional sales opportunities |
| Dealer/distributor partnership | Dedicated regional business development |

**My strongest recommendation:** Approach manufacturers with a request for a *manufacturer-sponsored demonstration partnership*, not an unconditional free-equipment request. Offer a defined promotional commitment, such as a 6–12 month pilot campaign, with agreed deliverables and reporting. Do not promise exclusive brand promotion unless the manufacturer offers suitable commercial benefits.

You can send this email separately to solar panel manufacturers, inverter and battery manufacturers, wind turbine manufacturers, and hybrid system manufacturers. Tailoring the equipment request to each manufacturer's products will improve your chances of receiving a serious offer.
