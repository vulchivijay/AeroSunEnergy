## My recommendation

For AeroSun Energy, the best approach is to make this a **controlled project checklist system**:

- Every project gets a unique project ID.
- Every phase has an assigned responsible person.
- Every completed phase requires evidence or documentation.
- Critical safety and approval stages cannot be bypassed.
- Failed inspections generate corrective actions and retesting.
- Final handover is permitted only after all mandatory acceptance criteria are met.

This will help AeroSun maintain consistent installation quality across solar, wind and hybrid projects as the business scales.
