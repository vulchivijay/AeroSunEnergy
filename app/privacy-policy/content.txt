2. **Refunds:** Define your actual refund processing timeline and cancellation deductions.
3. **Delivery:** Confirm whether you offer delivery across India and whether transportation and unloading are included in quotations.
4. **Warranty:** Publish actual manufacturer warranty periods for the products you sell. Do not use one universal warranty period for all equipment.
5. **Installation:** Clearly distinguish between equipment supply, installation, commissioning, and maintenance.
6. **Payments:** Define accepted payment methods and your advance-payment or milestone-payment arrangements.

**Legal recommendation:** Have an Indian lawyer review these documents before publication, particularly the cancellation, refund, warranty, liability, and consumer-rights provisions. These templates should reflect your actual business practices; publishing a policy does not by itself establish legal compliance.
