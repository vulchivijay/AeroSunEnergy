Battery storage may be included in solar, wind, and hybrid renewable energy installations.

Battery performance depends on:

- Battery chemistry and model.
- Nominal and usable capacity.
- Charge and discharge cycles.
- Depth of discharge.
- Temperature.
- Charging and discharging rates.
- Battery management system.

Nominal battery capacity is not necessarily equal to usable energy delivered to connected equipment.

Customers must follow manufacturer instructions for safe battery installation, operation, and maintenance.
